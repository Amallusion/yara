    Two things worth knowing

    Check the label

    Most of what's here is plant-based by nature. These vary by brand, so read the ingredients:

    • Bread and tortillas: milk or milk powder.
    • Pasta: egg, especially fresh pasta.
    • Peanut butter and spreads: honey or whey.
    • Stock cubes and spice mixes: chicken fat or chicken flavor.
    • Sweets and jellies: gelatin.

    Nutrients to plan for

    • Vitamin B12 isn't reliably found in plant foods.
    • Vitamin D deficiency is common in Saudi Arabia.
    • Iron from beans and lentils is absorbed better with vitamin C.
    • Calcium: tahini, almonds, and fortified plant milk. Omega-3: chia seeds and walnuts.

    General information, not medical advice.
